An AI assistant on Telegram, for you and your team

Griffin connects your agents to Telegram (and Bale) as a bot or through your own account. You talk to it in Saved Messages, colleagues message it directly, and scheduled jobs post their results to the chats where people already are.

Griffin on GitHub Quick start

Bot or your account

Pair a Telegram or Bale bot with a one-time code, or sign in your own account so the assistant can read and answer where you ask it to.

Threads with colleagues

A small classifier decides whether a colleague's message is real work; thanks and small talk stay yours. A thread closes with the result.

Permissions per person

Each person gets a profile with their own access and tool limits; anything irreversible comes to you as a button.

Reports and charts in the chat

Scheduled jobs deliver answers, files and charts as images to any chat you choose.

Persian first

Right-to-left aware and written for natural Persian, with English everywhere else.
